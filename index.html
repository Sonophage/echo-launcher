Extensible Console Handheld Operator

Your handheld, as a console.

One crossbar for everything: games from the emulators you already have, Android apps, music, video, photos and books, all from a controller or by touch.

Download the APK Power on ↓ Android 10 or newer · Free · Side-loaded
01Power on

It starts with a ring of light.

Hold A and the game’s art shrinks into the ECHO ring and spins there as a disc. With the launch sound the ring opens like a lens and the game fills the screen.

02Controls

Every button has a job.

Controller ▸ Type picks the button glyphs drawn in the footer and every hint. Every control has a touch equivalent. Pick a button to see what it does.

03The crossbar

Categories across. Everything else down.

Each category is a column. Media columns list the apps that belong to them first, then their own library rows, ending with a Folders row for the folders they scan.

Use ← → to move along the bar
04Games

Every console you own, on one card each.

Point ECHO at your Games folder and it adds a system for every console that has games, with the emulators you already have. A focused game fills the background with its own art, its title and facts beside the tile; X opens its details.

05Launch

Hold

Games and apps launch when you hold A until the ring fills, so a stray press never launches anything. GameBoot then plays one of two launches: Disc, where the cover becomes a spinning disc, or Lens, where the ECHO ring opens over the game. Try it: hold the A orb, or the A key.

06The shelf

Artwork that looks like a shelf.

Box covers fetched from ScreenScraper, SteamGridDB, IGDB and Steam, shown as VHS cases. A game's cover keeps its own shape on the case's ribbed plastic, so square Game Boy boxes and tall Switch covers both show whole.

07Media

Built-in players for everything else.

08Profile

Your games, hours and achievements.

The Profile tab shows your name and picture, your games, hours and achievements, what you played last with its achievement progress, and your Steam, RetroAchievements and Discord accounts. Library overview opens your whole library at a glance.

Steam RetroAchievements Discord
09Two screens

On a dual-screen handheld, both screens.

On a handheld like the AYN Thor, the XMB stays on top and the bottom screen follows it: the focused game's details, or the game you are playing with a Resume button. Last Played, the App Drawer, Search and Settings open down there, so the XMB never leaves the top.

Tap a screen to give it the controller. B hands it back to the XMB.
Swap puts the XMB on the bottom screen and the companion on top, and ECHO remembers it.
DS and 3DS emulators keep the bottom screen while a game runs.
Quick settings ▸ Screens switches between Dual and Single.
Typing with the controller? Inlay, a free gamepad keyboard for Android handhelds, works well with it.
10Make it yours

Almost everything is adjustable.

Controller glyphs, touch, layout, colours, icons, wallpaper, sounds and boot all have a setting, and most of it previews live on the crossbar. Quick settings can strip the footer down to the A button and the filter.

Bring ECHO home.

You need Android 10 (API 29) or newer, and the emulators you want to use, installed separately. ECHO launches them and does not emulate anything itself.
